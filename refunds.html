Refund Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

We want you to pay only for what actually works for you. This is how refunds work. All refunds are paid back into your SwiftPlug wallet in naira and appear in your wallet history.

1. If we cannot provide your number

When you buy, the price is taken from your wallet first. If our provider cannot supply the number, you are refunded automatically and in full. If you bought several numbers and only some could be supplied, you are refunded for the ones that could not.

2. Cancel a one-time number yourself

If you change your mind or no code arrives, you can cancel a one-time number straight from its card in your workspace, as long as no message has been received on it. Providers usually ask for a short wait of about 2 minutes after buying before a cancel is accepted. A successful cancel refunds the price to your wallet automatically, once.

3. One-time numbers that receive nothing

If a one-time number expires without receiving any message, contact Support within 24 hours and tell us the number and the service. We check the provider record, and if no message was received and it was not caused by misuse, we refund it.

4. When a code arrives

Once a message has been received on a number, the number has been delivered and is not refundable. This includes cases where the app later rejects the code or the number, because we cannot control third-party platforms (see section 5 of the Terms).

5. Longer-term numbers

  • You can ask for a refund within 24 hours of buying, if the number has not received any messages or calls.
  • After that, the current period is not refundable, but you can stop future renewals by contacting Support before the renewal date.
  • If a number stops working because of a fault on our side or our provider's, we will fix it, replace it, or refund the unused part of the period.

6. Wallet top-ups

  • Wallet funds are for buying numbers on SwiftPlug. Once credited, a top-up is not paid back out to your bank except where the law requires it.
  • If you were charged by Paystack but your wallet was not credited, contact Support with your Paystack reference and we will sort it out. Our system also checks payments with Paystack automatically.
  • If you want to close your account, ask Support about any remaining balance. We handle these requests case by case and may deduct payment fees, and we can refuse where we suspect fraud.

7. Social Boost orders

  • If the supplier cannot place your order, or it is cancelled, you are refunded automatically and in full.
  • If an order is only partly delivered, the undelivered part is refunded automatically to your wallet.
  • Orders that were delivered as described are not refundable. This includes orders sent to a wrong or private link, and cases where a platform later removes or reduces the engagement (see section 6 of the Terms).

8. Not refundable

  • Numbers used in breach of our Terms, or on accounts suspended for abuse.
  • Numbers that were delivered as described but that a third-party platform did not accept.
  • Social Boost orders that were delivered as described.
  • Balances obtained by fraud or reversed payments.

9. How to ask

Use the Support button on any page, and include the number, the service, and the time you bought it. We review requests as quickly as we can.
