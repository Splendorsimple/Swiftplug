Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This explains what personal data SwiftPlug collects, why, and what your choices are. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a securely hashed password, or basic profile details from Google if you use Google sign-in.
  • Wallet and payment records: top-up amounts, purchases, refunds and Paystack payment references. Card details are entered on Paystack and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Number activity: which numbers you rent, for which service and country, and the messages and call records those numbers receive.
  • Usage and security data: purchase logs used for rate limits and abuse prevention, and basic technical data such as IP address that our hosting and security tools process.
  • Support messages: anything you send when you contact us through the Support options.
  • On your device: your login session and your light/dark theme choice, kept in your browser. We do not use advertising trackers.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: sign you in, hold your wallet, provision numbers and show you incoming messages (performance of our contract with you).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and keep purchase limits fair (our legitimate interests).
  • To meet financial record-keeping and other legal duties.
  • To answer support requests.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Vercel: hosting of the website.
  • Paystack: payment processing.
  • Google: only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Number providers: when you buy a number we send our provider the country, service and plan needed to supply it. We do not send them your email or name.

Some of these companies operate outside Nigeria, so your data may be processed in other countries. We only use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

4. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open. Payment and wallet records are kept for as long as financial and legal rules require. Messages and call records are kept as long as needed to provide the service and resolve disputes, and you can ask us to delete them.

5. Security

Access to data is restricted so that customers can only see their own records, and sensitive provider keys are kept on the server side only. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

6. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to certain uses, or receive a copy. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To make a request, use the Support button and tell us which right you want to use. We may need to confirm it is really you first, and we may keep records we are legally required to keep.

7. Children

SwiftPlug is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you on the site.
