1. What SwiftPlug does

SwiftPlug lets you rent phone numbers from third-party telecom and verification providers. There are two kinds:

  • One-time (disposable) numbers receive text messages for a short window, normally about 20 minutes, and are then released.

You rent the use of a number. You never own it. Numbers are supplied by our providers on demand, so availability and prices differ by country and service and can change.
